## Description
This summer I interned at K-Scale Labs for a month. I worked on a teleoperation system for their humanoid robot that allowed control of the robot from a VR headset. I touched all parts of the robotics stack, including modifying firmware, training RL policies, writing custom inverse kinematics, and working on camera calibration and rendering.
