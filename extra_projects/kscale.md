## Description
This summer I had the great opportunity to work at K-Scale Labs for a month. The opportunity really came out of nowhere. I was living with my parents and working on personal projects during the summer between graduating from UC Irvine and starting my masters degree at UCSD, and joined a few robotics discord servers to get project inspiration and chat with others in the open-source robotics community. One day I was checking the server and saw Ben (the CEO) posting about issues they were having with getting good calibration for the fisheye cameras on their robot. I had worked on this exact problem for my A2RL team before, and had been working on a library to make calibration easier as one of my summer projects, so I replied with some tips and an offer to come over to the K-Scale house and help them out. At the time I was just thinking it would be cool to visit and see them working on the robot while also getting a chance to work out the kinks in my calibration library.
